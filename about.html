About MoneyForge

A local AI content studio. Your GPU does the generating, you pay once, and nothing is metered.

MoneyForge is a desktop application for Windows that generates images, video, multi-scene movies, music videos, and narrated faceless shorts on your own computer's graphics card. When the content is ready, it can caption it and post it to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, using accounts you connect yourself. Pinterest support is built and waiting on Pinterest's developer approval; X support is planned.

Why it exists

Most AI video and short-form tools run in the cloud and charge per generation: credits, monthly caps, or "unlimited" plans with fair-use limits. That pricing follows from paying for someone else's GPU time. If you already own a capable GPU, the per-generation cost is close to zero, so MoneyForge is sold as a one-time license with no credits and no generation caps.

What it does

  • Images with multi-character face consistency, so the same cast stays recognizable across shots.
  • Text-to-video and image-to-video through a full local video-generation pipeline.
  • Movies: up to six shots chained into one continuous sequence.
  • Music videos cut to a track's lyrics or beat.
  • Faceless reels: AI-written scripts, narration, captions, and background music.
  • Voice cloning through your own ElevenLabs account, plus free built-in narration voices.
  • Scheduling: pick a cadence and MoneyForge generates and posts on its own.

Your content stays on your machine

Generation runs entirely on your computer. Nothing you create leaves it unless you choose to post it, or you turn on an optional cloud add-on such as premium voices. The app needs a network connection for license activation, first-time model downloads, and posting. See the Privacy Policy for details.

MoneyForge

MoneyForge is a local desktop application that generates images, video, movies, music videos, and narrated shorts using your own computer's GPU.

Once you've generated content, MoneyForge can publish it directly to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, and X, using accounts you connect yourself.

MoneyForge is a one-time purchase — there are no subscriptions, credits, or generation caps.

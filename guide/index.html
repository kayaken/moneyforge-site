Limits worth knowing first

MoneyForge runs entirely on your own graphics card, so the size of each job is limited by what one card can do. These are the real limits in version 1.0.2:

WhatLimitWhy it matters
Video (one clip)Up to 7 secondsFor anything longer, use Movie.
MovieUp to 6 scenes of 5 seconds (about 30 seconds) per jobLonger films: make several movies and join them in a video editor.
Music video, Full AI video modeUp to 5 AI scenes per songThe scenes repeat to fill the rest of the song. Lyric-card mode has no scene limit.
Characters in one jobUp to 4Pick them from your saved characters.
Graphics card12 GB VRAM for video, movie and AI music videos; 6 GB for imagesSetup only offers the video models on 12 GB+ cards.
Generation timeAbout 1 to 2 hours per 5 seconds of AI video on an RTX 3060 12 GBNewer cards are faster. Images take seconds to a few minutes.
Jobs at onceVideo-type jobs run one at a timeImage and reel jobs can share the card if you turn on GPU concurrency.

Personal and Pro

Both licenses include every media type (image, video, movie, music video, reel), characters and batch prompts. Pro adds 4K and 8K image upscaling, 720p video and 4K video export. Controls that need Pro show a padlock. A new install runs as a 7-day trial until you activate a license.

Images and styles

Images are the fastest thing MoneyForge makes, and the starting point for animating, characters and thumbnails.

Make an image

  1. Open Create, pick an image template or ✦ Start from scratch, and choose Image.
  2. Choose an aspect ratio and Image quality. 1080p is the default. 4K and 8K (Pro) add an upscaling step that enlarges the finished image to that size.
  3. Optionally pick a Style preset and characters.
  4. Write the prompt and click Generate ✦.

If you've added a Gemini key or installed the free local AI model, MoneyForge scores each image and automatically retries low-scoring ones (up to 3 times).

Batch

Use Batch ✦ to make one image per line. Good for a set of thumbnails or a storyboard.

Animate an image

In the Gallery, click Animate on any image. Create opens as a Video with that image as the first frame. Write a motion prompt, such as "slow push-in, hair moving in the wind". Needs the video models (12 GB card).

Styles

A style is a reusable look added to every prompt. MoneyForge ships with about 11 built-in styles (marked ⚙), including Cinematic realism and Anime / manga.

To make your own, open Styles and click + New style:

  • Name.
  • Look & feel: words added to every prompt, for example "watercolor, soft pastel palette, paper texture".
  • Avoids: things to keep out, for example "photorealistic, harsh shadows".
  • Quality: Faster, Balanced or Best.

Remix

In the Gallery, Remix opens Create with all the settings of an earlier result filled in, so you can change one thing and try again.

Media type

Music videos

Upload your song and MoneyForge builds a video around it, keeping your real audio. Choose fast lyric cards or AI-generated scenes.

Use music you own or have the rights to. The music video is the only media type that keeps your song's own audio.

Step 1: Audio Source

On Create choose Music video. Then pick a source:

  • Single track: Audio track (.mp3, .wav, .flac, .m4a, .ogg). Optionally add an .lrc file (lyrics with timings) or paste plain lyrics, one line per lyric.
  • Playlist: several audio files at once, with optional .lrc files matched by file name.
  • Album (ZIP): a .zip with numbered tracks, optional matching .lrc files and an optional album_art.jpg. Album art handling: use it as the cover only, or animate it as the opening of track 1.

Step 2: Prompt & Generation

Prompt mode:

  • Single prompt: one description for the whole song; scene changes land on the beat.
  • Lyrics-driven: one scene per lyric line, drawn from that line's words. Songs without lyrics fall back to beat cuts.

Step 3: Album Options (playlists and albums)

Per-track makes one video per song. Full album (continuous) also joins them into one long video; tick Show title card at each track boundary to add song titles between tracks.

Recipe: a music video for one song

  1. Choose Music video, aspect 16:9 (YouTube) or 9:16 (Shorts, TikTok).
  2. Upload the song and its .lrc file if you have one. No .lrc? Paste the lyrics, or skip lyrics.
  3. For a first version, use Lyric cards with a style and a prompt that sets the mood, such as "neon city at night, rain, cinematic".
  4. Add captions in Advanced if you want the lyrics styled; choose a 🎤 style for word-by-word highlighting.
  5. Happy with the look? Try Full AI video with the same prompt for moving scenes, and leave it running overnight.
Tip

Getting an .lrc file: many music players and lyrics sites export them, or you can make one with a free LRC editor. Lines look like [00:12.40] first lyric line.

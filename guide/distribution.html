Publishing

Posting and scheduling

MoneyForge makes everything on your PC. Connect your YouTube channel once, then post from the Gallery, auto-post when a job finishes, or let a series generate and post on a schedule. Other platforms switch on as each one approves MoneyForge.

Posting status

Posting from inside MoneyForge needs each platform to approve MoneyForge's posting access, so it is switching on one platform at a time. Until a platform is ready, the Distribution page shows a "coming soon" card for it.

Connect an account

Open Distribution and connect a platform that is ready. You sign in on the platform's own page; MoneyForge never sees your password.

Upload it yourself in the meantime

For a platform that isn't ready yet, upload the file yourself. It takes a minute:

  1. If the video was made for another shape, use Repurpose → Create platform variant in the Gallery first (see Gallery).
  2. Click Download on the item in the Gallery.
  3. Open the platform's app or website and upload the file as a new post.

Public tunnel

Pinterest videos are fetched from a web address, so MoneyForge needs a temporary public link to your computer. On Distribution, click Start tunnel before posting a video to Pinterest, and Stop tunnel when you're done. Change the default admin password before you start it.

Post

  • From the Gallery: Distribute on any item.
  • Automatically: tick platforms under Auto-distribute on completion on Create. Posts go out within about 5 minutes of the job finishing.

Schedule a post

In the Distribute window, under When, choose Schedule and pick a date and time. YouTube (up to a year ahead) and Facebook (up to 29 days ahead) publish it themselves at that time, so your computer can be off by then. Times must be at least 15 minutes away.

  • A scheduled YouTube video stays private until its publish time, then goes public.
  • Facebook can only schedule public posts. Set Privacy to Public, or choose Post now.
  • Platforms that can't schedule post straight away. The window lists which ones.
  • Scheduled posts show as Scheduled with their time under Recent distribution jobs on the Distribution page.

Suggested times

Suggest a time offers three upcoming times. YouTube and Facebook don't share "when your audience is online" with other apps, so these are MoneyForge's own estimate:

  • General starting points: weekday afternoons for YouTube (before the evening viewing peak), weekday mornings and lunchtime for Facebook, later mornings at weekends.
  • Your own results: once a YouTube channel has at least 8 videos posted from MoneyForge, the weekday where they did best is suggested first ("Your last 12 videos did best on Thursdays").
  • Spacing: times within 2 hours of another scheduled post are skipped.

If most of your viewers live in another country, set Settings → Audience time zone. Suggestions are then picked in their time zone and still shown in yours.

Auto-scheduler series

A series writes, generates and posts reels on its own. Under Auto-scheduler click + New series and set the niche, Series name, optional Topic, Aspect ratio, Voice and Narration language, then How often (Twice daily, Daily, 3x per week, Weekly), an optional Post at time, and where to post. Pause, resume or delete a series at any time.

With Post at set (for example 21:00, or use Suggest a time), MoneyForge starts making each reel about 2 hours early. YouTube and Facebook then publish it exactly at that time; other platforms post when the time arrives. Leave Post at empty to post each reel as soon as it's ready.

Every scheduled reel passes a content-safety check first. It needs a Gemini key or the free local AI model; without one, scheduled reels are skipped rather than posted unchecked. Your computer must be on for a series to run. Scheduled uploads count toward YouTube's daily upload limit (see Posting status above), so on a busy day a scheduled post may have to wait until the next day.

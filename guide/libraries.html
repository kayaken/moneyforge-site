Your assets

Libraries and brand kits

Libraries hold your own media, reused across jobs. A checkbox on Create stays greyed out until its library has at least one item.

Upload only media you own or are licensed to use.

LibraryFieldsUsed by
Background MusicName, Audio file, Mood, TagsMusic under reels, videos and movies. Auto-match by keyword picks by tags and mood.
Sound EffectsName, Audio file, TagsEffects at reel scene cuts and repurposed-clip starts.
Stock FootageName, Video file, TagsB-roll reels: scenes are matched to clips by tags.
Whiteboard AssetsName, SVG file, TagsPer-scene drawings in whiteboard reels.

Good tags make matching work: describe what's in the clip or the mood of the track ("city, night, traffic", "calm, piano").

Brand kits

A brand kit puts your branding on every reel, music video and repurposed clip. Open Brand Kits and set:

  • Logo with Watermark position and Watermark opacity.
  • Watermark text and its position (for example your @handle).
  • Intro clip or Intro text, and Outro clip or Outro text.

Choose the kit under Brand kit in Create's Advanced section.
